# 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing global sales data to uncover valuable business insights related to:

* Sales performance
* Customer behavior
* Product demand
* Seasonal trends
* Operational risks and cancellations

The project was built using Python for data analysis and Excel for dashboard visualization.

---

# 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis aimed to answer several important business questions:

* Which months generated the highest sales?
* Which countries contributed the most revenue?
* What are the best-performing product lines?
* Are there products with high cancellation rates?
* Which markets drive the business growth?
* How does customer behavior vary across products and regions?

# 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

* Converted ORDERDATE to datetime format
* Removed unnecessary columns with excessive missing values
* Created new features such as:

  * Month
  * Year
* Checked and handled missing values

# 📈 Key Insights

## 🔥 Sales Trends

* November recorded the highest sales volume.
* Sales increased significantly from 2003 to 2004.
* Year 2005 contained incomplete records, making comparisons less reliable.

---

## 🌍 Country Performance

* USA was the strongest market by both sales and order volume.
* Spain and France also showed strong performance.
* Some smaller markets generated higher average order values.

---

## 🛒 Product Performance

* Classic Cars was the top-performing product category.
* Vintage Cars and Motorcycles also generated strong sales.
* Trains showed consistently weak performance.

---

## ❌ Cancellation Analysis

* High-demand products also experienced higher cancellation rates.
* USA recorded the largest number of cancellations due to high order activity.
* Cancellation patterns may indicate operational or supply chain issues.
